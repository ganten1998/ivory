Tangent

Tangent watches what you play and names it: the chord, the same notes on a guitar neck, and where they sit on the circle of fifths.

Two things can be installed, and you choose on the next page:

  • The application goes to /Applications. It opens its own window and finds a MIDI keyboard by itself.
  • The VST3 plugin goes to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, where every DAW looks. It reads the notes on the track it is on and produces no audio — put it on a MIDI or instrument track, not an effect slot.

Both can be installed together and neither needs the other.
