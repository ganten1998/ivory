## Description

> Tangent is free. This page is for people who want to pay for it anyway.

**What Tangent is.** Plug in a MIDI keyboard and Tangent draws all 88 keys in
real time while a weighted-scoring engine names what you are playing: plain
triads through altered dominants, rootless jazz voicings, slash chords and
scales. Then it shows you the same notes four more ways: on a guitar neck where
a player would actually put their fingers, on the circle of fifths, on a
Tonnetz, and **engraved on a staff**.

**New in 4.x: the sheet music panel.** What you are playing, written properly:
six clefs, all fifteen key signatures, correct accidental spelling for the key
you are in, `8va` when the notes climb too far for ledger lines, and letter
names inside the noteheads so a beginner can read it on day one. Stack several
clefs at once and every staff shows every note, so a viola player and a cellist
can read the same chord in their own clefs, side by side, off one keyboard.

**The chord readout lives on the staff now, and it names its runners-up.** Not
just "C6" but "C6, or Am7". A chord that is two things at once is a fact about
harmony, and most tools can only tell you one of them.

**Rearrange it with the number keys.** The theory band is four panels and `1`
to `4` toggle them. Press a number twice and that panel moves to the end, so
the same four keys both choose what is showing and arrange it. Turn them all
off and the band collapses and gives the space back.

**It records.** Load a VST3 instrument, play, and get a `.wav`, a `.mid` and a
composited `.mp4` in one take: the app's own display as the video with your
camera in the corner, or a 9:16 cut for reels. Count-in in any time signature,
metronome, VU meters, level faders.

**It runs in your DAW.** VST3 plugin with the same display, on a MIDI or
instrument track. It makes no sound of its own; it is a monitor.

**It is offline.** No account, no telemetry, no phoning home for chord
definitions. It works on a plane.

macOS 11+ (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 10+, Linux (glibc 2.32+, ALSA,
Wayland or X11).

### What the key gets you

A supporter key turns on a small heart in the corner and nothing else. **Every
feature is in the free build.** There is no locked panel, no watermark, no
nag. Keys have no expiry and cover every version, past and future.

You are buying the thing being made, not a thing you receive.
