## Description

> Tangent is free. This page is for people who want to pay for it anyway.

**What Tangent is.** Plug in a MIDI keyboard and Tangent draws all 88 keys in
real time while a weighted-scoring engine names what you are playing: plain
triads through altered dominants, rootless jazz voicings, slash chords and
scales. Then it shows you the same notes four more ways: engraved on a staff,
on a guitar neck where a player would actually put their fingers, on the circle
of fifths, and on a Tonnetz.

**It writes music.** The sheet music panel engraves what you are playing:
six clefs, all fifteen key signatures, accidentals spelled correctly for the
key you are in, `8va` when the notes climb too far for ledger lines, and letter
names inside the noteheads so a beginner can read it on day one. Stack several
clefs at once and every staff shows every note, so a viola player and a cellist
can read the same chord in their own clefs, side by side, off one keyboard.

**The chord readout names its runners-up.** Not just "C6" but "C6, or Am7". A
chord that is two things at once is a fact about harmony, and most tools can
only tell you one of them.

**Rearrange it with the number keys.** The theory band is four panels and `1`
to `4` toggle them. Press a number for a panel that is already showing and it
moves to the end, so the same four keys both choose what is up and put it where
you want it. Turn them all off and the band collapses and gives the space back.

**It records the window.** Load a VST3 instrument, play, and get a `.wav`, a
`.mid` and a composited `.mp4` from one take. The video is the window: the same
panels in the same places at the same sizes, with your camera where the window
already puts it. What you were looking at is what the file contains. Count-in
in any time signature, a metronome, VU meters, five instrument slots.

**It runs in your DAW.** VST3 plugin with the same display, on a MIDI or
instrument track. It makes no sound of its own; it is a monitor.

**It is offline.** No account, no telemetry, no phoning home for chord
definitions. It works on a plane.

macOS 11+ (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 10+, Linux (glibc 2.32+, ALSA,
Wayland or X11).

### What the key gets you

**Nothing.** That is not modesty, it is the specification.

Every feature is in the free build. There is no locked panel, no watermark, no
nag, no trial. The supporter heart that used to be the one thing a key switched
on is drawn for everybody now, in whatever colour they pick.

What a key does is put your name on the short list of people who paid for
something they already had. Keys have no expiry and cover every version, past
and future, so if anything is ever worth charging for you will already own it.

You are buying the thing being made, not a thing you receive.
