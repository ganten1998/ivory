# Gumroad product page for 5.0

Paste-ready copy for <https://ganten.gumroad.com/l/ivory>. The product is the
**supporter key**, not the app: the app is free and always has been, and the
page has to say so before it says anything else or the whole thing reads as a
paywall with the price filed off.

## What changed for 5.0, and it is a repositioning

Through 4.x this page sold a MIDI monitor with good theory panels. That is what
it was. It is not what it is now.

Two things changed it. **The sheet music panel** made it something you can
teach from: several clefs at once, every staff showing every note, correct
spelling for the key. **The recorder** made it something you can publish from:
one press writes the audio, the MIDI and a video of the window itself, so the
thing a student or a viewer needs to see is the thing that comes out of the
file with no second setup to maintain.

So the page leads with what somebody would USE it for, and the feature list is
underneath as the evidence. A monitor is a thing you glance at. A teaching and
recording tool is a thing you build a lesson or a channel on, and those are
different sentences.

**And the key gates nothing.** The heart used to be the one thing it switched
on. It is drawn for everybody now, and it will stay that way at least until 5.0
is final, so the page says exactly that. A page promising a feature Tangent no
longer gates is a page that lies to somebody who paid.

## Description

> Tangent is free. This page is for people who want to pay for it anyway.

### What Tangent is for

Plug in a MIDI keyboard and play. Tangent names the chord, engraves it on a
staff, shows you why it works on three theory diagrams and where it sits on a
guitar neck, all in real time. Then it records the whole thing.

That combination is the point. It makes Tangent two things:

**A teaching tool.** Everything a student needs to see is on screen at once and
in one another's terms: the notes under your hands, the name of what they make,
the notation for it, and the geometry that explains why it sounds like that.
You can put several clefs up at the same time and every staff shows every note,
so a violist and a cellist read the same chord in their own clefs, side by side,
off one keyboard. `1` to `4` rearrange the panels mid-lesson without a menu.
Teach it a chord name it does not know and it is yours from then on.

**A recording and performance tool.** One press writes a `.wav`, a `.mid` and a
composited `.mp4`, and the video is the window itself: the same panels in the
same places, with your camera already placed in it. There is no scene to build,
no overlay to align, no second layout to keep in step with the first. What you
were looking at is what your audience sees. It comes out 16:9 with no crop.

If you teach, that is a lesson recorded while you give it. If you make things,
that is a finished take with the theory visible in it, from one keypress.

### What is on screen

- **All 88 keys** in real time, from any MIDI keyboard.
- **The chord, named**, by weighted scoring rather than a lookup table: triads
  through altered dominants, rootless jazz voicings, slash chords and scales.
  It names the runners-up too, so "C6, or Am7" instead of one answer to a
  question that has two.
- **Sheet music.** Six clefs (treble, bass, alto, tenor, and the octave-down
  treble and bass a guitarist and a double bassist actually read), all fifteen
  key signatures, accidentals spelled correctly for the key you are in, `8va`
  when the notes climb too far for ledger lines, and letter names inside the
  noteheads so a beginner can read it on day one.
- **Three ways of seeing why**: the circle of fifths with each key shaded by
  how much of what you are playing fits it, a Tonnetz with your triads lit as
  triangles, and harmonic triangles for the functional reading.
- **A guitar neck** with fingerings where a player would actually put them,
  playable as an input in its own right, with barres, capo and every tuning.

### What comes out

- **A take is the window.** One press, and you get the audio, the MIDI, and an
  `.mp4` of exactly what was on screen with your camera in it.
- **Five VST3 instrument slots**, each with its own level and its own editor,
  so the sound in the recording is your instrument and not a placeholder.
- **A real transport**: record and stop, a pair of VU meters, and metronome and
  input faders you can reach while your hands are busy.
- **A count-in** in any time signature including 6/8, kept out of the file or
  put in it, your choice.
- **The MIDI file is a real MIDI file**, so a take can be re-rendered, notated
  or edited long after the video is made.

Video works on macOS, Windows and Linux. macOS encodes natively with nothing
to install; Windows and Linux use `ffmpeg`, which is one install away and which
a take names for you if it is missing. A take without it still writes its audio
and its MIDI rather than failing quietly.

### And it stays out of the way

**It runs in your DAW.** VST3 plugin, same display, on a MIDI or instrument
track. It makes no sound of its own; it is a monitor.

**It is offline.** No account, no telemetry, no phoning home for chord
definitions. It works in a classroom with no wifi and on a plane.

macOS 11+ (Apple Silicon and Intel, signed and notarized), Windows 10+, Linux
(glibc 2.32+, ALSA, Wayland or X11).

### What the key gets you

**No features.** That is not modesty, it is the specification.

Every feature is in the free build. There is no locked panel, no watermark, no
nag, no trial, no export limit. The supporter heart that used to be the one
thing a key switched on is drawn for everybody now, in whatever colour they
pick, and it will stay that way at least until 5.0 is final.

What you do get:

- **You hear about new versions.** Buying puts you on the list that gets a mail
  when a release lands, written by the person who made it and describing what
  actually changed. It is the only mailing list Tangent has, there is no other
  way onto it, and it is used for releases and nothing else.
- **Whatever gets made for supporters later.** Nothing is promised and nothing
  is being withheld today. But if anything is ever made specifically for the
  people who paid, a key is how it would reach you, and keys have no expiry and
  cover every version past and future, so you would already have it.

That is the honest list. You are buying the thing being made, not a thing you
receive.
